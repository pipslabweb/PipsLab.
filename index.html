How the cashback works

The broker provides cashback based on eligible trading activity. The cashback amount is calculated according to the broker's rebate program and the eligible trading volume generated by your account.

Trade like normal

Continue trading with your broker account on MT4, MT5, or the broker's own trading platform. Your normal trading process does not need to change.

Your trading volume is recorded

The broker tracks the eligible trading volume generated by your account and calculates the cashback according to the applicable rebate rate.

Receive your cashback

Cashback generated from eligible trades is paid according to the broker's cashback arrangement and payout schedule.

Getting your first cashback

Four steps, most of it handled by the broker's own account opening flow.

01

Use the channel’s broker referral link

Use the relevant broker referral link shared through the PipsLab channel, and confirm the offer and account eligibility before registering.

02

Verify your identity

Complete the broker's standard identity check with a government ID. This is required by the broker before any account can trade live.

03

Start trading

Once your account is approved, trade your normal strategy on MT4, MT5, or the broker's own platform.

04

Receive your cashback

Eligible rebates are calculated and paid daily.
